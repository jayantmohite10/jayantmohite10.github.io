01 / ABOUT

Connecting observations.
Enabling decisions.

My research lies at the intersection of Geospatial AI, Earth Observation and environmental intelligence, with a focus on transforming scientific advances into scalable real-world solutions.

I conceptualized the Sky–Earth Convergence approach, bringing together satellite, UAV and ground observations with domain-aware AI to enable Monitoring, Reporting and Verification (MRV) across agriculture, forestry and biodiversity. My work spans precision and regenerative agriculture, soil and forest carbon, deforestation, ecosystem assessment and environmental risk prediction.

Across these domains, I aim to advance scalable, explainable and increasingly autonomous geospatial intelligence that bridges Earth Observation science and actionable sustainability decisions.

05 RESEARCH AREAS
01 / CROP INTELLIGENCE

Multi-Sensor GeoAI for Crop Characterization, Health & Yield Intelligence

SAR–optical fusion, deep learning and crop simulation for acreage mapping, sowing and phenology, pest/disease stress, and yield estimation. Includes label-efficient sugarcane mapping and hybrid rice cross-pollination risk.

Sentinel-1/2Crop yieldPhenology
Related research: Mohite et al. (2016, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2023, 2024, 2025)
02 / WATER INTELLIGENCE

Multi-Scale Earth Observation for Soil Moisture, Water Stress & Irrigation

Integration of satellite microwave and optical products, proximal observations and UAV hyperspectral sensing for moisture estimation, spatial downscaling, agricultural drought and crop-water-stress analysis.

SMAPSARHyperspectral
Related research: Mohite et al. (2017, 2019, 2021, 2022)
03 / SUSTAINABILITY MRV

EO-Based MRV of Regenerative Practices & Agricultural Carbon

Time-series detection of cover crops, remote sensing-based soil organic carbon and bulk-density assessment, and multisource ensemble learning for scalable agricultural carbon intelligence.

Soil carbonCover cropsMRV
Related research: Mohite et al. (2022, 2023, 2025, 2026)
04 / FOREST INTELLIGENCE

LiDAR–EO Fusion for Forest Biomass, Deforestation & Wildfire Risk

GEDI and multisensor satellite analytics for forest structural attributes and aboveground biomass. Complementary research investigates time-series vegetation signals for pre-ignition wildfire risk estimation.

GEDI LiDARForest AGBFire risk
Related research: Mohite et al. (2024, 2026)
05 / FOUNDATIONAL METHODS

Advanced Spatial–Temporal Learning, SAR–Optical Fusion & Autonomous GeoAI

Cross-cutting research on cloud-free vegetation indicator estimation, low-label mapping, spatial downscaling, multi-temporal feature extraction and fusion across sensor modalities. Recent technology development extends toward agentic Geospatial AI for EUDR deforestation assessment and biodiversity monitoring.
